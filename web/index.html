NewSync

Synchronized Lyrics Extension

Brings Apple Music-style synchronized lyrics, word-by-word timing, and animated album artwork to YouTube Music and supported web players.

Privacy Policy

  • ✓ No Data Collection: NewSync does not collect, track, or share any personal information, listening history, or browser telemetry.
  • ✓ Local Processing: All preferences, custom CSS, and cached lyrics remain strictly saved on your own device (`storage.local` / `IndexedDB`).
  • ✓ Direct Requests: Network requests are only made directly to public lyrics APIs to fetch lyrics for the playing track. No analytics or tracking tags attached.
